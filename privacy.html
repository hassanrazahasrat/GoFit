Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

Who we are

GoFit ("the app") is a fitness and nutrition planning application that runs in your browser and inside our mobile apps for iOS and Android. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Data we collect

  • Account information — your email address and a password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), or your name and email if you sign in with Google.
  • Profile and body details — the name you choose, age, sex, height, weight, target weight, activity level, calorie goal, and whether a clinician should guide your plan. You enter these yourself in the setup quiz.
  • Training data — your training preferences, generated workout plans, exercises completed, sets, weights, personal records, and session history.
  • Nutrition data — meal plans, foods you log, calorie and macro totals, and water intake.
  • Progress data — weigh-ins, body measurements, and daily step counts.
  • Health platform data (mobile apps only) — if you grant permission, the iOS app reads step counts, workouts, and body weight from Apple Health, and the Android app reads the same categories from Health Connect (which may include data written by Samsung Health or Google Fit). This data is used only to fill in your steps, weight, and workout history inside GoFit.
  • Usage analytics — anonymous event counts (for example, which screens and buttons are used) sent through Google Analytics to help improve the app. No advertising identifiers are collected.
  • On-device storage — your plan, logs, theme, and session are also cached locally on your device so the app works offline.

How we use your data

  • To create and personalise your workout, calorie, and meal plans.
  • To track your progress, personal records, and history over time.
  • To sync your data across devices when you are signed in.
  • To keep the app working reliably and to improve it.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with data brokers.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication (email/password and Google sign-in). Your account credentials and synced data are stored on Supabase infrastructure.
  • Google — provides Google sign-in and Google Analytics.
  • Apple / Google Health platforms — the mobile apps read health data only after you grant permission in the system health app. GoFit never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS.

Health disclaimer

GoFit provides general fitness and nutrition information, not medical advice. Plans are estimates generated from the details you provide. If you are pregnant, injured, have a medical condition, take medication, or a clinician has asked you to follow a specific diet or exercise programme, talk to them before following any GoFit plan. The app does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional care.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists so your plan and history can sync across devices. You can sign out at any time from the You tab, which removes the session from the device. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us using the link below and we will remove it from our systems.

Children

GoFit is not directed at children under 13, and calorie and training targets in the app are intended for adults (18+).

Your choices

  • You can use the app without an account — your data then stays only on your device.
  • You can decline health platform permissions; the app still works with manual entry.
  • You can clear the app's local data through your device settings at any time.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date. For privacy questions or deletion requests, reach us through our GitHub repository.
