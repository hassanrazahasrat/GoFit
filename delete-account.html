Delete your account & data

You can request deletion of your GoFit account and all associated data at any time.

What gets deleted

When your request is processed we permanently remove:

  • Your account credentials and sign-in identity
  • Your profile details (name, age, sex, height, weight, goals)
  • Workout plans, sessions, exercises, sets and personal records
  • Nutrition logs, meal plans and water/step history
  • Weight, measurements and any synced health data

Data stored only on your device (if you never signed in) is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

How to request deletion

  1. Open a new issue on our GitHub repository titled "Account deletion request".
  2. Include the email address you used to sign up for GoFit so we can locate your account.
  3. We will confirm and complete the deletion, usually within 7 days.
Request deletion on GitHub

Before you delete

If you only want to sign out or remove the app from one device, use Sign out on the You tab — your cloud data stays safe and you can sign back in anytime. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
