Calorie, macro, BMI, and training numbers are estimates, not guarantees. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting a new diet or exercise programme — especially if you are pregnant, have a heart condition, injury, chronic illness, are taking medication, or have been advised to follow a specific programme by a clinician.

If you told the app that a clinician should guide you, treat every plan as a draft and confirm it with them before following it.
